# Incident Response Playbook – Ransomware Healthcare Clinic

This playbook defines the structured response actions taken during a ransomware incident affecting a healthcare organization, aligned with the NIST Incident Response Lifecycle.

---

## 1. Identification

* Multiple employees reported inability to access systems and medical records.
* Ransom notes appeared on affected endpoints indicating file encryption.
* Incident classified as **Ransomware / High Severity** due to operational and patient care impact.

---

## 2. Containment

* Immediately disconnected affected endpoints from the network.
* Disabled compromised user accounts associated with phishing emails.
* Temporarily shut down critical systems to prevent further spread.

---

## 3. Eradication

* Identified phishing emails and malicious attachments as the initial infection vector.
* Removed ransomware binaries from affected systems.
* Conducted malware scans on all endpoints and servers.
* Reset credentials for affected users.

---

## 4. Recovery

* Restored systems from verified clean backups where available.
* Gradually reconnected systems to the network after validation.
* Monitored systems closely for signs of reinfection.

---

## 5. Lessons Learned

* Employees require additional phishing awareness training.
* Email filtering and attachment sandboxing controls must be strengthened.
* Offline and immutable backups should be regularly tested.
* Incident response procedures should be reviewed and exercised periodically.

---

## Status

Incident response actions documented for portfolio and training purposes.
