# Incident Timeline – Ransomware Healthcare Clinic

This timeline reconstructs key events associated with the ransomware incident affecting a small healthcare clinic in the United States. Times are approximate and based on initial reports.

---

## Pre-Incident Phase

**Prior to Tuesday – Unknown Time**

* Targeted phishing emails were sent to multiple clinic employees.
* Emails contained a malicious attachment designed to deploy malware upon execution.

---

## Incident Detection and Impact

**Tuesday – Approximately 9:00 AM**

* Employees reported inability to access computer files, including electronic medical records.
* Business operations were disrupted due to loss of system availability.

**Tuesday – Shortly After 9:00 AM**

* A ransom note appeared on affected systems.
* The note stated that organizational files had been encrypted and demanded payment in exchange for a decryption key.

---

## Incident Response Actions

**Tuesday – Mid-Morning**

* Internal IT or technical staff became aware of a widespread security incident.
* Initial assessment indicated a ransomware attack.

**Tuesday – Late Morning**

* Systems were shut down to prevent further spread of the ransomware.
* External organizations were contacted to report the incident and request technical assistance.

---

## Ongoing / Unknown

* Extent of data exfiltration remains unknown.
* Status of backups and recovery capability requires further investigation.
* Regulatory and legal notification requirements are under review.
