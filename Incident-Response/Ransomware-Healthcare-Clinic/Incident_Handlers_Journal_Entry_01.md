# Incident Handler’s Journal – Entry 01

## Date

2026-02-01

## Entry Number

1

## Description

This journal entry documents a ransomware incident that disrupted the operations of a small healthcare clinic in the United States. Employees were unable to access medical records and essential systems due to widespread file encryption. A ransom note was displayed, demanding payment in exchange for a decryption key.

## Tool(s) Used

No security tools were directly used at the time of this initial journal entry. The entry is based on incident reports from staff and preliminary incident awareness.

## The 5 W’s of the Incident

### Who caused the incident?

The incident was caused by an organized group of unethical hackers known for targeting organizations in the healthcare and transportation sectors.

### What happened?

Attackers delivered ransomware through targeted phishing emails containing a malicious attachment, which, once opened, installed malware and encrypted critical organizational files.

### When did the incident occur?

The incident occurred on a Tuesday morning at approximately 9:00 AM.

### Where did the incident occur?

The incident occurred within the internal network and endpoint systems of a small healthcare clinic located in the United States.

### Why did the incident occur?

The incident occurred because employees received and downloaded a malicious email attachment, allowing attackers to gain network access and deploy ransomware.

## Additional Notes

It is unclear whether the organization had recent, offline backups available at the time of the incident. Further investigation is required to determine the extent of data loss, backup integrity, and potential regulatory reporting obligations.
