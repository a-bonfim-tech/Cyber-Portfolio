# Decision Record — DEC-INC-2026-01

## Context
Incident INC-2026-01 — potential credential exposure

## Decision

## GDPR Basis
- Art. 5(1)(f): confidentiality
- Art. 32(1)(b): ability to restore security

## Risk Considered
- Credential interception
- Persistence of compromised secrets

## Rejected Alternatives

- No action (rejected: unacceptable residual risk)

## Evidence Generated
- Timestamped credential rotation
- Network offline state confirmation
